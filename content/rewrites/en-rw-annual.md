Two words cause constant confusion in readings of Zi Wei Dou Shu, the Chinese system of Purple Star Astrology: the major cycle and the annual cycle. The major cycle, called the Da Xian (大限) in the Zhongzhou school, changes palace every ten years and sets the theme of a life stage. The annual cycle changes every year and works more like the weather of that year. This article separates the two and then uses the Bing-Wu year of 2026 and the Ding-Wei year of 2027 to show how annual transformations are read inside a chart.

## The ten-year cycle and the year

The major cycle's starting age depends on the Five Elements Bureau (五行局). A Water Two Bureau begins at age two, Wood Three at three, Metal Four at four, Earth Five at five and Fire Six at six, and the palace changes every ten years after that. Direction depends on the birth-year stem and gender: yang-year men and yin-year women move clockwise, yin-year men and yang-year women move counter-clockwise. Yang years end in the stems Jia, Bing, Wu, Geng or Ren, and yin years in Yi, Ding, Ji, Xin or Gui.

Whichever palace the major cycle reaches becomes the focus of that decade. Reaching the Wealth Palace (財帛宮) puts money at the center of ten years, and reaching the Spouse Palace (夫妻宮) puts partnership under the spotlight. It does not say that the decade will bring wealth or marriage, only that the area will draw attention.

An annual chart starts from the palace of the year's earthly branch. For 2026, a Bing-Wu year, the Wu palace becomes the annual Life Palace. For 2027, a Ding-Wei year, it is the Wei palace. The twelve palaces are then laid out in order, and the year's transformations are placed on the stars they affect. The key idea is that an annual transformation activates what is already in the natal chart. A Tian Tong that is quiet at birth lights up in a year of Tian Tong Hua Lu, and a Lian Zhen that is calm becomes visible in a year of Lian Zhen Hua Ji. The natal chart is the substance and the annual cycle is the use.

## What the Bing-Wu year of 2026 activates

The Bing-year transformations are Tian Tong (天同) Hua Lu (化祿), Tian Ji (天機) Hua Quan (化權), Wen Chang (文昌) Hua Ke (化科) and Lian Zhen (廉貞) Hua Ji (化忌).

Tian Tong Hua Lu enlarges comfort, goodwill and quality of life. The area where Tian Tong sits in your chart tends to feel easier this year. Tian Ji Hua Quan is the power of planning and control: a good time to redesign a process or learn something systematic. Wen Chang Hua Ke leans toward exams, documents and reputation. Lian Zhen Hua Ji is the one to watch. The Zhongzhou school, which this site follows, does not treat Hua Ji as a disaster. It treats it as a point of fixation. In a year like this one, that tends to mean over-demanding in love and refusing to loosen control at work. Knowing where the fixation lies is more useful than worrying about it. ## What the Ding-Wei year of 2027 changes

The Ding-year transformations are Tai Yin (太陰) Hua Lu, Tian Tong Hua Quan, Tian Ji Hua Ke and Ju Men (巨門) Hua Ji.

Tai Yin Hua Lu favors wealth and household accumulation, which suits steady long-term planning. Tian Tong Hua Quan continues the theme of the previous year and turns the blessing star from enjoying life to actively arranging it. Tian Ji Hua Ke recognizes thinking and study. Ju Men Hua Ji calls for care in communication and misunderstanding: spoken promises and suspicion between people become more common, so put important matters in writing.

Reading the two years together shows that Tian Tong and Tian Ji appear in both. One shared theme of 2026 and 2027 is therefore adjusting ideas and the rhythm of daily life. People whose natal Tian Tong and Tian Ji sit in different palaces will feel it in different parts of life.

Putting the cycles together, a reader can follow three steps. First read the natal pattern to see strengths and weak points. Then find which palace the current major cycle occupies, which gives the theme of the decade. Only then read what the annual transformations activate. Reversing the order makes it easy to treat one year's turbulence as a life event.

An example, entirely hypothetical: a reader called Kai, an invented figure and not a real person or a real chart, is in a major cycle sitting on the Career Palace (官祿宮). That makes work the focus for ten years. In a year when the annual transformation activates a star in the Career Palace, changes at work are especially worth noticing. If only the Sibling Palace is activated, while the cycle and natal chart point elsewhere, it is more likely a minor episode.

Three common misunderstandings are worth clearing up. Rankings such as "luck for the Year of the Horse" squeeze everyone into one zodiac sign, which has little to do with an individual chart. The same transformation produces opposite experiences depending on where the star sits at birth. Moving into a new major cycle is not a switch flipped on a birthday, and the transition usually takes a year or two. And annual transformations are trends, not verdicts, so preparing early beats explaining afterward.

Treat annual transformations as reminders, not judgments. A year of Tian Tong Hua Lu is a good time to improve quality of life, though not a reason to stop working, and a year of Lian Zhen Hua Ji is a chance to practice letting go, not a prediction that love will fail.
