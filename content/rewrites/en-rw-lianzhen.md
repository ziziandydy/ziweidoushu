Lian Zhen (廉貞) is a Northern Dipper star of yin fire in Zi Wei Dou Shu, the Chinese system of Purple Star Astrology. Classical texts give it two names that seem to pull in opposite directions: the "prisoner star" and the "secondary peach blossom star." The first suggests someone held in by rules and duty. The second suggests warmth that draws people in. Together they describe a character that wants badly to break free and is also easily moved, so the same person is often caught between self-discipline and strong feeling.

A small clarification first: Lian Zhen is not Mars. Huo Xing (火星), the star usually translated as Mars, is a different star among the six malefic stars, and it signals sudden temper. Lian Zhen's fire is closer to a furnace that stays hot: persistent, focused and strong. It is one of the fourteen major stars, and its concern is ambition and emotional depth.

## Six placements, six temperaments

Lian Zhen has one solitary position and five combinations with other major stars, and the differences between them are large. Alone in the Yin and Shen positions it is the purest form: demanding of itself, intense in feeling, calm and professional at work and much more sentimental in private. With Tian Xiang (天相) in Zi and Wu, the person looks easygoing and keeps strict principles, and may swallow frustration until it breaks out unexpectedly. With Tian Fu (天府) in Chen and Xu, ambition is wrapped in steadiness, which suits a patient climb through an institution. With Qi Sha (七殺) in Chou and Wei, love and hatred are sharp, effort is intense, and the person may choose extreme routes. With Tan Lang (貪狼) in Si and Hai, charm and talent combine with strong appetites, and this is the arrangement most easily moved by emotion and temptation. With Po Jun (破軍) in Mao and You, thinking is dramatic, life has large swings, and big breaks with the past are common; it also suits starting a business.

Two people who both say "I have Lian Zhen" can lead entirely different lives, one in a quiet administrative career and the other in a volatile start-up. The companion star matters more than the label.

## Hua Lu and Hua Ji: one side good, one side hard

Lian Zhen receives only two of the four annual transformations. In a Jia year it gets Hua Lu (化祿), and in a Bing year it gets Hua Ji (化忌).

Hua Lu on Lian Zhen tends to bring opportunity and popularity. Career helpers appear, and attention from others increases. Because this is a peach-blossom kind of luck, it should be read as relationship capital rather than pure financial luck. Used well it is a network, and used carelessly it becomes personal complication.

Hua Ji is the version of fixation. The Zhongzhou school, which this site follows, does not read it as simple misfortune. It reads it as the point where a person cannot let go. In a Spouse Palace (夫妻宮) it often shows up as excessive demands in love, and in a Career Palace (官祿宮) as an inability to loosen control over work. People born in other years may meet either transformation during an annual cycle, which then brings the same themes to the surface. Entrepreneurial drive comes with conditions. Lian Zhen is often described as an entrepreneur's star, and that statement needs qualifying. Its ambition is stronger than most major stars, and it is not afraid of hard work, so it has a real edge in early, difficult stages. Founding a company also needs financial judgment and the ability to manage a team. The solitary Lian Zhen and the Lian Zhen with Po Jun are usually good at the first part. Lian Zhen with Tian Fu or Tian Xiang leans toward the second.

In short, Lian Zhen has courage but not always patience. The dangerous moment in the early phase is putting in too much, too fast, because enthusiasm is high, and then running short of cash before noticing there was no exit plan. A better description than "suited to entrepreneurship" is "suited to entrepreneurship with someone else controlling risk." A hypothetical example: a founder called Mira, an invented illustration and not a real person, invests savings in a restaurant in her first month. Her chart shows the Po Jun combination, so the energy is there. What saves the business is a business partner with a steady temperament who insists on a three-month cash reserve. The Lian Zhen drive and the Tian Fu discipline need each other. ## Intensity in love

Calling Lian Zhen a secondary peach blossom star does not mean a complicated love life. A more accurate description is emotional concentration. These people love deeply and expect the same level of response. When they feel undervalued, they withdraw and turn cold, and that is not a change of heart. It is the prisoner star's self-protection, the reflex to lock the door after being hurt.

A partner who notices the silence beginning can help by asking directly: "Do you feel I'm not taking you seriously?" That one question is often more effective than waiting for the Lian Zhen person to process it alone. For the Lian Zhen person, the practical step is to name the hurt within a day, instead of letting it harden into distance.
